# Evaluation work — retired

- The project evaluated whether reference Skills helped agents solve bounded synthetic scientific tasks and whether shorter descriptions improved selection.
- The saved collection contained 263 top-level result records across pilot, baseline, selective-package and experimental-draft runs. These were local synthetic evaluations, not official benchmarks.bio scores.
- A completed comparison used seven tasks and five repeats per condition with deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash and twelve Skills per task.
- Without Skills, 18 of 35 attempts passed. Full descriptions produced 19 of 35 passes and observed Skill references in 17 attempts. Short descriptions produced 24 of 35 passes and references in 26 attempts. These three matched groups recorded no infrastructure errors.
- Most of the short-description improvement came from the fine-mapping task. The small experiment did not establish a general causal effect, and it had no upstream-Skill arm at the selective-package size.
- The harness used isolated execution, hidden graders and synthetic task generators. Source code and task definitions remain as historical software; trajectories, result rows, copied run workspaces, outputs and evidence archives were retired.
- Sam ended the effort on 2026-11-07. No new models, experiments or scientific workflows were run for this cleanup.
