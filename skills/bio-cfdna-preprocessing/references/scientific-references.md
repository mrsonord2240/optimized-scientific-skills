# Scientific references

- Snyder MW, Kircher M, Hill AJ, Daza RM, Shendure J. 2016. Cell-free DNA
  comprises an in vivo nucleosome footprint that informs its tissues-of-origin.
  *Cell* 164:57-68. Source for the 167 bp mode, roughly 10.4 bp periodicity,
  35-80 bp transcription-factor/CTCF footprints, and nucleosome-positioning
  basis of the coordinate-deduplication trap. DOI: `10.1016/j.cell.2015.11.050`.
- Underhill HR, Kitzman JO, Hellwig S, Welker NC, Daza R, Baker DN, Gligorich
  KM, Rostomily RC, Bronner MP, Shendure J. 2016. Fragment length of
  circulating tumor DNA. *PLoS Genetics* 12:e1006162. Source for the shorter
  ctDNA distribution and BRAF V600E mutant fragments around 132-145 bp versus
  wild-type fragments around 165 bp. DOI: `10.1371/journal.pgen.1006162`.
- Mouliere F, Chandrananda D, Piskorz AM, Moore EK, Morris J, Ahlborn LB, Mair
  R, Goranova T, Marass F, Heider K, et al. 2018. Enhanced detection of
  circulating tumor DNA by fragment size analysis. *Science Translational
  Medicine* 10:eaat4921. Source for 90-150 bp and 250-320 bp enrichment
  windows. DOI: `10.1126/scitranslmed.aat4921`.
- Burnham P, Kim MS, Agbor-Enoh S, Luikart H, Valantine HA, Khush KK, De
  Vlaminck I. 2016. Single-stranded DNA library preparation uncovers the
  origin and diversity of ultrashort cell-free DNA in plasma. *Scientific
  Reports* 6:27859. Source for recovery of ultrashort mitochondrial and
  microbial cfDNA by ssDNA preparation. DOI: `10.1038/srep27859`.
- Newman AM, Lovejoy AF, Klass DM, Kurtz DM, Chabon JJ, Scherer F, Stehr H,
  Liu CL, Bratman SV, Say C, et al. 2016. Integrated digital error suppression
  for improved detection of circulating tumor DNA. *Nature Biotechnology*
  34:547-555. Source for complementary barcode and in-silico suppression.
  DOI: `10.1038/nbt.3520`.
- Kennedy SR, Schmitt MW, Fox EJ, Kohrn BF, Salk JJ, Ahn EH, Prindle MJ, Kuong
  KJ, Shen JC, Risques RA, Loeb LA. 2014. Detecting ultralow-frequency
  mutations by Duplex Sequencing. *Nature Protocols* 9:2586-2606. Source for
  two-strand concordance and the duplex error floor. DOI:
  `10.1038/nprot.2014.170`.

For fgbio option semantics, consult the documentation and `--help` output of
the installed fgbio release rather than assuming flags are stable across major
versions.
