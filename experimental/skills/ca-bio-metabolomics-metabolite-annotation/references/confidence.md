# Evidence levels and cautions

GPTomics source distinguishes same-method authentic-standard confirmation (level 1), probable reference-spectrum or diagnostic structural hypotheses (2a/2b), ambiguous candidates or classes (3), formula (4), and an unresolved feature (5). These labels require evaluation of the complete evidence rather than a score cutoff alone; MSI and Schymanski terminology are not perfectly interchangeable. Exact-mass and spectral matches may not resolve isomers or stereochemistry. Group adduct and in-source fragment families to avoid counting one compound multiple times, and carry ambiguity into downstream analysis. The source's entropy, COSMIC and CCS numeric thresholds are benchmark-dependent rather than universal. Source versions include matchms 0.33.1, SIRIUS 6 and MetFrag 2.6.1; verify current CLI and schemas.
