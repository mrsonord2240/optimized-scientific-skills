# Assay interpretation

GPTomics source describes authentic standards, matched stable-isotope internal standards, weighted calibration, back-calculated relative error, qualifier/quantifier ratios, matrix factor, recovery, carryover and precision. Co-elution and matched chemistry help address matrix effects but do not guarantee complete cancellation. A high R-squared or low CV alone does not prove accuracy. A concentration outside a validated range or an unconfirmed identity needs an explicit status and reason. The source cites ICH M10 and SANTE thresholds as historical examples; applicability, current requirements and the complete protocol must be verified by qualified reviewers. Its synthetic example derives LLOQ from accuracy alone and does not establish a validated limit. Its example LOD formula mixes raw area and ratio slope; do not use without a dimensionally consistent validated method. These scripts are normalization-only drafts and have not been executed.
