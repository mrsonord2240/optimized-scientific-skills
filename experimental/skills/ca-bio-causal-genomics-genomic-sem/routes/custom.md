# Custom factor models

This source provides input-dependent examples, not a file-based runnable entry point for ESEM, userGWAS or higher-order models. Prepare full LDSC S and V and adapt `references/advanced-models.md` to the specified traits and identified model. Check model identification and report rotation sensitivity. userGWAS whole-model chi-square is not Q_SNP.

Done when identified model estimates and fit diagnostics are reported.
