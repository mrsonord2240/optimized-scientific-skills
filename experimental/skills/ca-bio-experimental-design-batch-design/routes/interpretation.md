# Correction and design review
A perfectly aliased condition and batch cannot be separated from the observed design. Include known batch in an appropriate inference model where estimable; cleaned matrices are for visualization in the source workflow. Avoid blindly adjusting mediators or colliders. Incomplete blocks, pooling and demultiplexing, run-order randomization and correction-method choices are discussed in [design guidance](../references/design-guidance.md). Method execution for ComBat, RUV and single-cell integration needs the appropriate downstream workflow.
