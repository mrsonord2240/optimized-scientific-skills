# Statistical assumptions

GPTomics source distinguishes transformation from scaling and highlights Pareto versus unit-variance sensitivity, paired or nested experimental units, dependence among features and closure effects. BH controls FDR under its applicable dependence conditions, not all possible correlations; report the testing family and do not equate feature counts with independent metabolites. A difference of log means is a log geometric-mean ratio. Supervised separation can arise in high dimensions without signal; model selection and preprocessing must be included in validation. Source Q2 and VIP cutoffs are heuristics, not guarantees. A permutation p-value alone does not establish external generalization. Source versions are historical; verify APIs.
