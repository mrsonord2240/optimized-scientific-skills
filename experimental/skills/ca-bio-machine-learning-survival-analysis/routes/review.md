# Validation and alternatives
C-index measures ranking and cannot establish calibration. Evaluate out of sample using suitable censoring-aware discrimination, time-dependent AUC, Brier score against a training-set Kaplan-Meier baseline and calibration at prespecified horizons. Define follow-up and censoring support, avoid post-baseline exposure leakage and use landmarking or an appropriate time-varying design when needed. Fine-Gray, random survival forests, deep models and full nested survival validation require study-specific workflows; see [methods](../references/methods.md) and [failure modes](../references/failure-modes.md).
