# Interpretation and provenance

Derived from GPTomics `bio-chipseq-allele-specific-binding` (MIT). ASB compares alleles within a sample; caQTL and bQTL refer to population associations. Sequence-model predictions are counterfactual predictions, not measured ASB. For ordinary autosomal interpretation flag or exclude imprinted loci, treat female chrX separately, and account for or exclude copy-number-altered regions. Personalized alignment does not eliminate those confounders. Restrict tests to appropriate peaks and retain exclusion reasons, depth, replicate agreement and REF/ALT orientation. Report the assembly, bias method, genotype source, filters, sex/chrX handling, imprinting and blacklist resources, correction source, model, parameters and multiple-testing or posterior criterion.

References: Rozowsky et al. 2011, Molecular Systems Biology 7:522, doi:10.1038/msb.2011.54; van de Geijn et al. 2015, Nature Methods 12:1061, doi:10.1038/nmeth.3582; Kumasaka et al. 2016, Nature Genetics 48:206, doi:10.1038/ng.3366; de Santiago et al. 2017, Genome Biology 18:39, doi:10.1186/s13059-017-1165-7; Mayba et al. 2014, Genome Biology 15:405; Chen et al. 2016, Nature Communications 7:11101.
