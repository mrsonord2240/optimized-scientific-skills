# Covariate-weighted FDR
~~~sh
Rscript scripts/ihw_safe.R tests.csv pvalue mean_expression weighted.csv 0.05
~~~
The covariate must be informative and independent of p-values under the null. The source helper uses five bins and up to three child-process attempts before a labeled BH fallback; inspect the printed method and preserve it with output. A source-observed solver crash is environment-specific, and installed APIs must be checked.
**Done when:** weights' assumptions, method actually used and output are recorded.
