# Filtering, dependence and coverage
Independent filtering is justified only when the filter is suitably independent of the test statistic under the null. Define the family before selection. Confidence intervals reported only for selected hits may need false-coverage-rate adjustment; the source gives a construction and assumptions in [methods](../references/methods.md), but does not provide a standalone general-purpose interval pipeline. The source's GWAS threshold is a convention, not universal across ancestry and test families.
