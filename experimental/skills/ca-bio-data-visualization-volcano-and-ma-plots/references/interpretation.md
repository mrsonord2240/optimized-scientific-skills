# Interpretation and source caveats

GPTomics source recommends examining low-count effect instability and using an appropriate shrinkage method when fitting DESeq2 results; apeglm uses a coefficient, while ashr can accept a contrast. Shrinkage is not guaranteed to reduce every absolute estimate. A threshold on adjusted p-values is distinct from a raw-p cutoff; do not label a raw-p horizontal line as FDR. Multiple-testing control depends on the adjustment procedure and assumptions. Missing adjusted p-values can result from filtering and should be counted, not silently converted to nonsignificant results. Labels selected by combined rank emphasize both effect and significance, while prespecified labels answer a different question. Capping should be explicit and visibly marked; clipping alone hides points. Visual MA patterns are diagnostic prompts, not proof of their cause. The source's broad claims about limma effect shrinkage and automatic diagnoses of stripes or asymmetry require independent method-specific verification. Source package versions are historical; confirm local APIs.
