# Interpretation and alternatives
TWAS, FOCUS, colocalization and cis-eQTL MR ask distinct but overlapping questions. Assess LD, tissue and ancestry mismatch, co-regulation and prediction-panel coverage; a TWAS hit alone does not establish a causal gene. Review [failure modes](../references/failure-modes.md), [reporting](../references/reporting-and-review.md) and [triangulation thresholds](../references/decision-framework.md). Exclude or separately analyse the extended MHC as described in failure modes.
UTMOST, MOSTWAS, kTWAS, EpiXcan, TIGAR-V2, individual-level PrediXcan, splicing and cell-type workflows require external models and method-specific inputs not supplied in the source as complete file-based pipelines. Adapt validated workflows after reviewing [method comparison](../references/algorithmic-taxonomy.md), [tissue selection](../references/tissue-and-model-selection.md) and [escalation](../references/escalation-and-reconciliation.md).
