# Ranking and null assumptions

GPTomics source recommends signed variance-calibrated rankings such as a Wald statistic or moderated t, unique IDs and decreasing order. Raw p-values lose direction; tied or rounded statistics can make order arbitrary. Weight exponent, gene-set size, annotation version and permutation null alter results. Preranked gene permutations do not preserve the sample-level correlation structure; matrix-based competitive or self-contained tests address different nulls and have their own assumptions. NES sign indicates the end of the ranked contrast, not necessarily pathway activation. Leading-edge genes are contributors, not independently validated drivers. Do not confuse BH-adjusted p-values with another tool's empirical FDR. Source package versions are historical; check installed signatures, seed and actual engine. GSVA scores are not direct measurements of activity.
