# Frequency review and bulk data
Use an assembly-matched source and distinguish raw AF from filtering allele frequency. Missing or undefined FAF95 is not evidence of absence and cannot automatically establish PM2. Disease-specific thresholds, inheritance, penetrance, coverage, QC and applicable VCEP rules require qualified assessment. Source formulas, dataset comparisons, SV/CNV and mitochondrial context are retained in [historical methods](../references/source-methods.md) and [usage](../references/usage-guide.md); release and criteria claims are unverified.
The source Hail example is a starting sketch, not a ready validated cohort filter: confirm table schema, missingness, allele normalization, reference build, privacy and approved environment before adapting.
