# Select a method
**Source-incomplete:** Method selection is conditional guidance rather than a standalone executable workflow. Consult [method catalog](../references/method-catalog.md) for design, assumptions and failure modes, and use the canonical bio-crispr-screens-mageck-analysis, bio-crispr-screens-bagel-essentiality, bio-crispr-screens-drugz-chemogenomic or bio-crispr-screens-copy-number-correction as appropriate.
Review screening QC and contrast comparability, then use [consensus](consensus.md) only where methods answer the same question. BAGEL BF and FDR thresholds are not equivalent calibrations; sign-correct MAGeCK neg|score before correlating with BAGEL BF.
Done when the selected method, comparison and limitations are documented.
