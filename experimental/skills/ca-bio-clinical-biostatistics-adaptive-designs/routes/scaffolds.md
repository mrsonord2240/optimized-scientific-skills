**Done when:** the planned adaptation has an explicit decision rule, inputs, control mechanism, information firewall and a trial-specific validation plan. This route requires adaptation and does not execute a trial design;.
Promising-zone SSR, enrichment, platform graduation and response-adaptive randomisation are source-incomplete scaffolds. Specify conditional power, adaptation thresholds and caps, original combination weights and IDMC output for unblinded SSR; pre-specify subgroup selection and closed testing for enrichment. For platform and RAR rules, specify drift adjustment, allocation floors, timing and simulations. These are not implemented by the packaged examples. Read [sequential and enrichment](../references/group-sequential-ssr-enrichment.md) and [platforms and RAR](../references/platform-rar-dose-finding.md).
