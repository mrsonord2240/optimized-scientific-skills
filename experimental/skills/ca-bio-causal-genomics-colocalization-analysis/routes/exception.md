Use the hg38 gate on the locus. MHC chr6:25-35 Mb requires an HLA-aware method or exclusion; chr8:8.1-11.9 Mb requires inversion conditioning or exclusion. For window sensitivity read `references/lead-snp-swap.md`; for low power and LD mismatch read `references/failure-modes.md`; for reporting read `references/reporting.md`.
