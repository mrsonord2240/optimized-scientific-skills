# Examine infinite-sites behavior
~~~bash
python scripts/infinite_sites_plot.py run1/out.txt run2/out.txt
~~~
Input MCMCTree posterior summaries need at least three nodes with matching output format. The source fits HPD width against mean age through the origin and compares first and last runs; a single high R-squared or apparent slope stability does not prove a calibration floor. Check matching nodes, calibrations and chain convergence.
Done when fitted trend and prior/posterior limitations are reviewed.
