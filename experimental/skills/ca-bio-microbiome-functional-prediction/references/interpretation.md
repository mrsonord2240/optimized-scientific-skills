# Prediction limits

GPTomics source describes reference placement, hidden-state gene-copy prediction, predicted marker-copy normalization and pathway reconstruction. The result is predicted potential conditioned on the ASV table and reference genomes, not an independent measurement of gene carriage, expression or activity. It is not orthogonal corroboration of a taxonomic comparison. Reference coverage and placement failures affect what is represented; NSTI alone cannot establish accuracy or strain-specific gene content. Abundance and pathway coverage are distinct. Tax4Fun2, FAPROTAX and BugBase use different concepts and are not interchangeable; no standalone workflow for them is supplied here. Differential analysis of predictions retains compositional and model uncertainty. Source PICRUSt2 observations refer to 2.6.3; verify current output names and options. Related skills include `bio-microbiome-differential-abundance` and `bio-microbiome-amplicon-processing`.
