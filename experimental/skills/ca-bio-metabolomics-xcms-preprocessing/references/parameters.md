# Parameter and interpretation notes

GPTomics source distinguishes CentWave on centroided high-resolution data from matched-filter alternatives, obiwarp from anchor-based peakGroups alignment, and grouping after RT adjustment. Ppm should reflect measured across-scan scatter; peakwidth and grouping bandwidth depend on chromatographic data and alignment. Sample groups must match file order. Source starting values are guides, not universal thresholds. `chromPeakData(xdata)$is_filled` marks filled peaks, whereas remaining NA can represent no detected signal. Keep unfilled and filled representations and assess the per-feature filled fraction; filling can integrate noise and does not establish detection. CAMERA isotope/adduct grouping needs appropriate samples and validation. GC-EI deconvolution is outside this LC workflow; use `bio-metabolomics-msdial-preprocessing` when appropriate. Source cites xcms 4.x and MsExperiment APIs; verify installed signatures before use.
