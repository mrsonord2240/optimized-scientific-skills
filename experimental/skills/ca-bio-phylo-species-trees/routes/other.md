# Site-based or full Bayesian alternatives
~~~bash
paup svdquartets.nex -L svdq.log
~~~
Supply a reviewed NEXUS alignment and species taxpartition with a suitable SVDQuartets command block as in [method details](../references/other-methods.md). BPP and StarBEAST2 require model-specific control/XML files and calibration; source BPP template is illustrative, not a general setup generator. Check versions and convergence independently.
Done when data mapping, model and method-specific limitations are documented.
