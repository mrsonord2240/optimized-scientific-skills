# Interpretation and alternatives
The inference chain runs from enzyme transcript to protein, activity, flux, metabolite level, export and sensing; none is directly measured by this analysis. Transporters may be bidirectional, ambient RNA and group size can distort scores, and dissociated cells lack spatial context. Compare conditions with independent biological replicates and avoid treating significance in only one condition as proof of a difference. [Methods](../references/methods.md) describe scFEA, Compass and NeuronChat, which need separate data and validated workflows. Orthogonal metabolite or spatial evidence is needed for substantive claims.
