# Concepts and limits

GPTomics source distinguishes mass isotopologues from positional isotopomers, an MID from atom-weighted mean enrichment, and isotopic from metabolic steady state. Natural abundance, tracer purity and derivative atoms affect correction. A plateau in labeling alone does not establish metabolic steady state or uniquely identify flux. Classical and nonstationary flux models require appropriate atom transitions, assumptions and data beyond a single MID. The source describes IsoCor 2.2.4 and AccuCor 0.3.1.9000; APIs and resolution-specific correction models require local verification.
