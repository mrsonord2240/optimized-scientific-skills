# Electrostatic shape comparison

```bash
shaep -q query.mol2 target.mol2 -s aligned_hits.sdf similarity.txt
```

Supply prepared 3D MOL2 files and check their coordinates first. For Open Babel preparation and ESPSim alternative see `references/esp-similarity.md`; shape and electrostatic scores have different semantics.

Done when alignment, scores and charge assumptions are recorded.
