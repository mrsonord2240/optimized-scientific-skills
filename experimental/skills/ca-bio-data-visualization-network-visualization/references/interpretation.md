# Network interpretation

GPTomics source recommends reporting node and edge counts, directedness, components, missing attributes, encoding, layout and seed. Force-directed positions vary with initialization and settings and are not measured biological coordinates. Compare conditions with one layout computed on the union graph and shared scales; the source gives a recipe but no standalone comparison CLI. Label the top `min(N, max(15, ceil(0.02N)))` capped at 30, plus chosen nodes. A dense graph may need a declared filter, aggregation or adjacency matrix; no automatic adjacency plot is supplied. Hierarchical bundling needs a real backbone. Source lists NetworkX 3.6.1, matplotlib 3.11.2, PyVis 0.3.2, py4cytoscape 1.13.0, Cytoscape 3.10.4, igraph 2.3.0 and ggraph 2.2.2 as historical checks; verify installed APIs.
