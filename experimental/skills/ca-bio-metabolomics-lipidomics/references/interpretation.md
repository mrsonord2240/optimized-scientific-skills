# Evidence and quantification

GPTomics source distinguishes sum composition, chain-resolved underscore, sn-resolved slash and double-bond localization; a parser preserves a claim but cannot establish that the experiment supports it. Routine CID may not resolve sn or double-bond position. Ether/plasmalogen distinction and hydroxyl assignments require diagnostic evidence. In-source fragmentation can imitate lyso or diacyl species; retention-time and orthogonal checks can help, while direct infusion lacks chromatographic separation. Class-matched standards added before extraction help account for class-dependent recovery and response; within-class ratios do not automatically justify cross-class molar claims. Report calibration, missing standards, class failures, analytical confidence and uncertainty. Source lipidr and pygoslin version checks are historical; confirm current APIs. This material concerns research cohorts, not diagnosis or interpretation of an individual patient.
